Recent sales come from free public data published by the county property appraisers in Miami-Dade, Orange, Hillsborough, Pinellas, Lee, Volusia, Sarasota, Collier, Palm Beach, Seminole, Manatee, Marion, Lake, Hernando and Highlands counties. Older sales, and every other county, come from the Florida Department of Revenue tax roll, published through the Florida Geographic Information Office. Each sale shows which one it came from. The buyer shown is the buyer on the deed where the county lists one, otherwise the current owner of record. Lee and Marion list sale dates by month, and the state roll does too.
