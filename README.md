# Seller Offer Calculator

A one-page tool for pricing offers to Florida home and land sellers:

- **Comps:** nearby recorded sales on a satellite map, from the Florida Department of Revenue tax roll (via the Florida Geographic Information Office statewide parcel layer).
- **Cash offer:** maximum allowable offer for homes, and a low, target and high offer for land.
- **Owner financing:** up to three seller-financing options side by side.

Everything runs in the browser. Numbers you type are kept only in that browser.
